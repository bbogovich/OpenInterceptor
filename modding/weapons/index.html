    Missiles and mines¶

    Every missile class chooses how it steers (guidance) and how its warhead's damage lands (damage). Both are fixed engine behaviours (src/sim/guidance.rs); everything else — speeds, turn rates, damage, decoy counts, split distances — is data. Stage 2 item 2.10. Every key: Mod file reference › missiles.

    missiles:
  cluster_rocket:
    name: Cluster rocket
    guidance: cluster          # splits into six seekers near the target
    cluster: {missile: seeker, count: 6, at: 700m}
    impact_damage: 120
    proximity_damage: 50
  decoy_rocket:
    guidance: parallax         # two flares that draw enemy rockets
    decoys: 2
  permeator:
    damage: bypass_shields     # straight to armour and hull

    Guidance¶

    guidance: Behaviour
    homing (default) Turns towards its locked target at turn_rate.
    dumbfire Never steers.
    sidestep Its own thrusters: turns twice as hard inside 600 m of the target and weaves, so it is hard to dodge.
    parallax Homing; at launch releases decoys (default 2) that enemy missiles may chase instead of the launcher.
    cloaked_wait Dropped behind the launcher; drifts to a stop and waits, hidden, until a hostile comes within trigger_range (default 800 m), then strikes.
    cluster Homing; near the target (cluster.at, default 600 m — or when half its fuel is spent) it splits into cluster.count (default 6) submunitions of cluster.missile, which carry on to the target.
    doppelganger Launches a decoy copy of the launcher's ship: a short-lived, fragile double that enemy ships and missiles may chase instead.

    Decoys. A decoy is a harmless missile flying away from its launcher. An enemy missile homing on the launcher, within 2.5 km of one of its decoys, may become lured (80 % chance per second) and from then on chases the nearest decoy; when the decoys are gone it returns to its target.

    Every missile still detonates on impact, on a near miss (the proximity fuse: inside proximity_radius and moving away) or when its fuel (duration) runs out. Its blast is the explosion effect it names (Looks) — size in metres, with the effect's sound — or a 25 m, 0.8 s fireball.

    Damage modes¶

    damage: How the warhead's damage lands
    normal (default) On the shield facing that was hit, then armour, then hull.
    bypass_shields Ignores shields: straight to armour and hull (the original's Permeator).
    enveloping Spread over both shield facings, as if the whole surface were hit.

    Point defence¶

    A ship or object with point_defence carries automatic guns that shoot at the nearest hostile missile in range — decoys included, since the guns cannot tell them apart:

    objects:
  guard_station:
    point_defence: {range: 1.2km, rate: 4, chance: 40%, energy: 3}
    Key Default Meaning
    range 800 m Reach.
    rate 3 Shots per second.
    chance 35 % Chance a shot destroys the missile, rolled on the world's seeded generator (battles replay identically).
    energy 4 Energy per shot, from the ship's capacitor; no energy, no shot.

    Each shot draws a tracer and plays the point_defence event sound. Point defence is fitted to the Patton, the X-COM freighter and the bases (interceptor), and to the dreadnought and the stations (default); the numbers are derived (DEV-038).

    Mines¶

    An object with kind: mine and a behaviour whose state does do: mine waits until a hostile is within the state's range, then detonates its first missile rack as the warhead: an area blast reaching four times the warhead's proximity_radius (at least 100 m), dealing its proximity_damage falling off linearly with distance, enveloping — and the mine is gone. See Stations and modules › Mines for a complete example.

    In the two campaigns¶

    Campaign Missiles
    interceptor X-Winder (homing), Sidestep, Permeator (bypass_shields), Fusion, Enveloping torpedo, Parallax torpedo (2 decoys), Passive Cloaking torpedo (cloaked_wait), Doppelganger — the special behaviours follow the original's descriptions (derived); the Super Avenger's testbed load carries one of each.
    default Seeker rockets, atomic torpedoes, cluster rockets (Thunderbolt), decoy rockets (Meteor), Dominion space-mine warheads (dominion-raid).
