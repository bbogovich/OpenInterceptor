    Cutscenes¶

    A cutscene is a short film the engine plays with its own ships, effects and sounds: craft that fly keyframed paths, fire and explode on cue, cameras that follow them with cuts between, and captions (app 0.13.0, plan item MD2). It is a pack file, cutscenes/<id>.yaml, written by hand until the Cutscene Designer (MD6) edits it. It runs on the ordinary battle simulation at the fixed 60 Hz tick with a fixed seed, so it looks exactly like a battle and plays the same way every time.

    Requirements: R-MOD-033 – R-MOD-045 (several partly met; see Not built yet). Design (the full planned format, of which this page is the built part): Mod designer › The Cutscene Designer. Editor schema: schemas/cutscene.schema.json. How it plays: Architecture › Presentation › Cutscenes.

    For films made in another program, the original's Smacker videos play through the video(…) action and campaign.intro_video (below); other video files are plan item MD3.

    Where cutscenes play¶

    How Where it is set Then
    A new campaign's introduction campaign: {intro: <id>} The strategy screen.
    A story event the action play_cutscene(<id>) in any story action list (Story layer) Back to the strategy screen; the clock stays stopped.
    From the command line openinterceptor --cutscene <id> The launcher.

    The player skips a cutscene with Esc or Enter. Music stops while one plays.

    A worked example: the default campaign's intro¶

    A Guard patrol of three Comets meets a Dominion saucer over the Gatehouse station; the leader fires and the saucer explodes; the camera ends on the station.

    # resources/default/cutscenes/intro.yaml
id: intro
title: Perils of the Void
length: 20s
environment: {starfield_seed: 31, sun: [-0.4, 0.45, -0.8], environment: orbit}
entities:
  gatehouse: {class: guard_station, keys: {position: [[0s, [0, -150, -3200]]]}}
  lead:
    class: comet
    keys: {position: [[0s, [-260, 40, 900]], [5s, [-80, 60, -400]], [10s, [60, 90, -1500]], [16s, [300, 160, -2600]]]}
    actions: [[9s, fire_beams, 2.5s]]
  # left and right: two more Comets on parallel paths
  saucer:
    class: raider_saucer
    enter: 6s
    keys: {position: [[6s, [1800, 400, -3800]], [9s, [700, 180, -2400]], [11s, [260, 110, -1900]]]}
    destroy_at: 11s
cameras:
  wide: {position: [[0s, [-120, 70, 780]], [6s, [60, 110, -260]]], look_at: lead, fov: 50}
  chase: {attach: lead, distance: [[6s, 70], [10s, 40]], orbit: [[6s, [25, 10]], [10s, [10, 6]]], fov: 60}
  station: {position: [[11s, [-500, 260, -1200]], [20s, [-700, 320, -900]]], look_at: gatehouse, fov: 45}
cuts: [[0s, wide], [6.5s, chase], [11.2s, station]]
captions:
  - [1s, 5s, "The frontier, 2084."]
  - [5.5s, 9.5s, "Regent Vexor's saucers prowl the cosmium belts..."]
  - [14s, 19.5s, "Only the Interplanetary Guard stands in their way."]

    # resources/default/campaign/campaign.yaml
campaign:
  intro: intro

    Play it on its own with openinterceptor --no-original --cutscene intro.

    Coordinates and times¶

    • Positions are metres in the battle's world: X right, Y up, −Z forward (the direction a craft with no rotation faces). The origin is arbitrary; keep the scene within a few kilometres of it, as a battle would.
    • Times are seconds from the start, with a unit or without: 9s, 2.5s, 1min, 12.
    • Keys are [time, value] pairs; they are sorted by time when the file loads.

    The file¶

    Key Type Default Meaning
    id id required The cutscene's id (named by campaign.intro, play_cutscene, --cutscene). A later pack's file with the same id replaces it.
    title text the id Its title (for the designer and listings; not shown during play).
    length time 10s When it ends.
    environment.starfield_seed number 1 The stars, and the seed of the scene's simulation.
    environment.sun three numbers [-0.4, 0.45, -0.8] The direction of the sun.
    environment.environment id none (deep space) A battle environment: its sky, planet, nebula and lighting.
    entities map none The craft and objects, by id (below).
    cameras map none The cameras, by id (below).
    cuts [[time, camera], …] required with cameras Which camera is in use from each time on.
    sounds [[time, sound, volume], …] none Named event sounds (below).
    captions [[from, to, text], …] none Caption lines and when they show.

    Entities¶

    Key Type Default Meaning
    class id required Any ship or object class of the loaded packs (fighters, saucers, stations, asteroids). An unknown class is a load error.
    faction id the class's Its faction (this decides its radar colour and who its weapons may hit).
    enter time its first key's time When it appears, at its place on the path.
    leave time never When it disappears (quietly, without exploding).
    keys.position [[time, [x, y, z]], …] required (at least one) Its path. One key holds it still.
    actions list none Timed actions (below).
    destroy_at time never Shorthand for [time, destroy].
    harmless bool false Its bolts and missiles fire, hit and show their effects but do no damage (app 0.14.1).

    Paths. The keys are joined by a centripetal Catmull-Rom spline: the path passes through every key exactly at its time, curves smoothly between them, and — unlike a plain Catmull-Rom spline — never overshoots or loops at a sharp corner. Before the first key and after the last the entity holds its position. The speed follows from the keys: far-apart keys close in time make it fast.

    Facing. An entity faces along its path and banks into its turns (up to 60°, from how sharply the path bends). An entity with a single key faces straight ahead (−Z).

    How entities move. An entity is placed on its path every tick: the simulation never steers it, so the flight model (Realistic or Simple) and the AI do not apply. Its velocity is the path's, so bolts it fires and its engine glow look right.

    Actions¶

    Action Written Does
    Fire beams [time, fire_beams, seconds] (default 1s) Holds its beam trigger for that long: its own beams fire as in a battle.
    Missile [time, missile, <entity>] Targets that entity and fires its missile, as the battle does (the class needs a missile).
    Destroy [time, destroy] Explodes with the class's explosion, debris and sound.

    Each action calls the battle's own code, so bolts, hits, shield flashes, explosions, debris and their sounds are the battle's. Beams and missiles do real damage: a craft caught in another's fire may be destroyed before its own destroy_at. Mark the shooter harmless: true for a show of fire that hurts nothing (since app 0.14.1: its hits land, with their flashes and sounds, but do no damage), and use destroy for kills.

    Cameras¶

    A camera is fixed (keyframed positions) or attached to an entity.

    Key Camera Default Meaning
    position fixed required [[time, [x, y, z]], …], joined by the same spline as entity paths.
    look_at fixed the origin An entity id, or a point [x, y, z]. The horizon is kept level.
    attach attached — The entity it follows.
    distance attached — [[time, metres], …] behind the entity, linear between keys.
    orbit attached [0, 0] [[time, [yaw, pitch]], …] degrees around the entity, in the entity's frame (yaw 0, pitch 0 is straight behind).
    fov both 60 Vertical field of view in degrees (10 – 120).

    cuts switch cameras instantly. Before the first cut the first cut's camera is used; with no cameras at all, a fixed view from [0, 50, 300] looking ahead.

    Sounds and captions¶

    • sounds: [[time, name, volume]] plays one of the campaign's named event sounds — the sounds: section's explosion, big_explosion, shield_hit, hyperspace, mission_accomplished and the rest (Mod file reference › sounds) — at that volume (0 – 1, default 1). The entities' own weapons and explosions make their sounds anyway.
    • captions: [[from, to, text]] shows a line at the bottom of the screen, centred in the letterbox bar, in the skin's font, from from until to. Quote a caption that contains a colon or a comma.

    What the player sees¶

    Black letterbox bars cover the top and bottom tenth of the screen; captions sit in the bottom bar. When the cutscene ends (or is skipped) the next screen draws its own sky again.

    Videos¶

    The original's films are Smacker videos, played full screen and letterboxed (app 0.12.0):

    How Written
    A story event the action video(original:<file>.smk) (Story layer)
    A new campaign's introduction campaign: {intro_video: original:<file>.smk} — used when the campaign has no intro cutscene
    An ending endings: {<id>: {video: …}} (Story layer)
    From the command line openinterceptor --video <ref>

    A video reference may also be a pack path (video/<file>.smk); only Smacker files play. A pack path is looked for in every active pack, latest first (app 0.14.1). Enter, Esc or a click skips a video; its soundtrack follows the effects volume (app 0.14.1).

    Validating¶

    Cutscene files are read with every other pack file when the game or --check loads: a mistake is reported with the file and the key, for example default/cutscenes/intro.yaml: cameras.chase: no entity 'leed'. The editor schema schemas/cutscene.schema.json checks keys and types as you type. A campaign.intro that names no cutscene is a load error (campaign.intro: no cutscene 'x' (cutscenes/<id>.yaml), app 0.14.1). A play_cutscene id that does not exist is still reported only when it would play (cutscene x: no cutscene 'x' on standard error), and the game carries on without it.

    Not built yet¶

    From the design, not in app 0.13.0:

    • Rotation keys (slerp) and speed keys; entities only face along their paths.
    • Autopilot entities flown by the AI (aggressive), switching between scripted and autopilot, and formations of a group following a leader.
    • The actions chase, capture (tractor) and jump (hyperspace); harmless on a single action (it is per entity).
    • Sound files at times with fades, positions and music ducking; captions as text keys (they are plain text, not translated).
    • Camera height and look-offset keys, keyframed field of view, blended cuts.
    • Cutscenes as a mission's intro and outro; skipping from the tablet console or the pad's Start button.
    • A video: file in place of a cutscene, and video files other than Smacker (MD3).
    • Scrubbing and snapshots (the designer's seeking, MD6).

    Errors¶

    Message Cause
    …/cutscenes/x.yaml: needs an id No id.
    entities.e: needs keys: {position: [[time, [x, y, z]], …]} An entity without position keys.
    entities.e: needs a class / …/cutscenes/x.yaml: unknown class 'y' No class, or one the packs do not define.
    entities.e: actions[i]: 'y' is not fire_beams, missile or destroy An unknown action.
    entities.e: key i: expected [time, value] (also bad time, bad value) A malformed key.
    cameras.c: no entity 'y' attach or look_at names no entity.
    cameras.c: needs position keys (or attach:) A fixed camera without positions.
    cuts: no camera 'y' / cuts: needs at least one [time, camera] A cut naming no camera, or cameras without cuts.
    sounds: expected [time, sound, volume], captions: expected [from, to, text] Malformed lists.
    campaign.intro: no cutscene 'x' (cutscenes/<id>.yaml) campaign.intro names no cutscene (app 0.14.1).
