    Testbed¶

    src/app/: the interactive program — a small state machine of screens sharing one AppContext. Every stage adds scenarios here so its features can be play-tested. Plan: Stage 1; Stage 2 added the controls menu, the campaign switch, the wingman comms menu, system and hyperspace messages, and --screenshot; Stage 3 the missions, the debrief, radio voices (app 0.6.2) and the simulator (app 0.6.3).

    Architecture Overview¶

    stateDiagram-v2

    [*] --> FlightSession: --scenario id
    [*] --> ModelViewer: --model name
    Launcher --> FlightSession: Enter on a scenario (Next::Fly)
    Launcher --> FlightSession: Enter on a mission (Next::Mission)
    [*] --> FlightSession: --mission id

    ModelViewer --> Launcher: Esc
    Launcher --> AssetBrowser: Enter on Asset browser (Next::Assets)
    AssetBrowser --> Launcher: Esc

    Controls --> Launcher: Esc (save bindings.yaml)
    Launcher --> Simulator: Enter on Simulator (Next::Simulator)
    Simulator --> FlightSession: Enter on Fly (Next::Simulate, a modified mission)
    Simulator --> Launcher: Esc
    FlightSession --> FlightSession: Enter after a simulator debrief (Next::Simulate, same setup)
    Launcher --> Launcher: Tab or D (Next::Reload) reloads Content for another campaign or difficulty
    Launcher --> StrategyScreen: Campaign: new game / continue (Next::NewCampaign / LoadCampaign)
    StrategyScreen --> FlightSession: fly an interception (Next::CampaignBattle; the screen waits in AppContext::campaign)
    FlightSession --> StrategyScreen: debrief Enter/Esc, or leaving (Next::Strategy with AppContext::battle_report)
    StrategyScreen --> Launcher: Esc (saves first)
    FlightSession --> Launcher: Esc menu → L
    FlightSession --> FlightSession: Esc menu → K (controls menu inside the session)
    FlightSession --> FlightSession: Esc menu → R, or Enter after death
    Launcher --> [*]: Esc
    • app::run(options) loads Content, creates the Renderer, loads sounds (async), reads the bindings, then loops: the current screen's frame returns a Next, then next_frame().await. A start-up failure shows an error screen instead of crashing.
    • --screenshot <png> (with --scenario or --model) runs the starting screen for --frames frames (default 120), saves what was drawn as an opaque PNG and exits — a way to check a cockpit or model on several window sizes without playing.
    • Launcher (launcher.rs): the header names the loaded campaign and difficulty level; Tab (pad Y) switches to the next campaign (launcher::next_campaign) and D (pad X) to the next difficulty level (launcher::next_difficulty), each returning Next::Reload { campaign, difficulty }: the app reloads Content (the ruleset is rebuilt at that level), saves the choice in settings.yaml (game.campaign, game.difficulty), and shows any load error on the launcher. Entries: the model viewer, the asset browser, Controls, the Simulator (app 0.6.3), every scenario, then every mission (Mission: name, its briefing as the description; Missions). The list scrolls to keep the selection near the middle (Menu::first_visible, "^ more" / "v more"). Scenarios are sorted by order; the first scenario is selected at start, found by searching the entries (Entry::Scenario) rather than by a fixed index, so adding an entry above the scenarios does not move the start. For player: choose scenarios, Left/Right picks among playable ship classes, previewed as a turning model with name, description and stats. Keyboard, mouse (click to select, click again to start) and gamepad (D-pad, A/Start, Back to quit). Skipped-pack warnings and start errors are shown.
    • Model viewer (viewer.rs): every ship class's model, then every original mesh. Orbit with arrows or mouse drag, zoom with +/− or the wheel, PgUp/PgDn (or B/N) to change model, L to cycle LOD (auto, 0–3), Space to stop spinning, Esc back. The camera is fitted to each model's radius and never enters it; viewer::wheel_steps turns wheel movement into zoom steps. The near plane follows the orbit (viewer::near_plane: just in front of the model, at least 1% of the distance, never under 5 cm), so depth precision suits a 600 m station as well as a 2 m debris chunk. Mesh-debugging keys: W cycles Display::Solid / SolidWire / Wire (each original face's outline as 3D lines, pulled 0.3% towards the camera over a solid model, plus a dot on every vertex of faces towards the camera); [ / ] select a face (Shift ×10), outlined and numbered, with viewer::face_info on the info line (index, corners, texture, flags, overlay layer, UVs); I numbers every face towards the camera; O toggles Renderer::show_overlays (restored on Esc). D (class entries) cycles the class's looks (viewer::looks_of: intact, each damage level, each named state, and the debris pieces pulled apart from the centre), named on the info line (look_label). --model NAME opens the viewer on a model (viewer::find_entry: ship id or mesh name, whole names, case-insensitive).
    • Asset browser (assets.rs): every original texture, image (cockpits, HUD, menus, sprites) and sound, loaded exactly as the game loads them — so a pack override shows its replacement, and the entry is marked [overridden by <pack>]. Tab switches between Textures, Images and Sounds; Up/Down (Shift: ×10) or B/N move; Enter or Space plays a sound; Esc back. Images are drawn over a checkerboard so transparency is visible. Its frame is async because loading a sound for playback is (macroquad's API); the app loop .awaits it.
    • Controls (controls.rs): the controls menu component (ControlsMenu), used from the launcher and from the flight pause menu; see Input.
    • Missions in a flight session: FlightSession::start_mission builds the battle with MissionRun::start; after each tick MissionRun::update referees it and its lines go to the message display; when it ends, the verdict and computer voice come at once and the battle runs on for flight.debrief_delay (30 s; flight::debrief_due, a countdown, Enter to skip; a retreat skips it — app 0.7.4, from the user's playtest), then freezes behind the debrief (result, text, kills, losses, time, optional objectives, campaign actions; long text wrapped). Enter (pad A) flies it again, Esc or L returns to the launcher; the pause menu's R also restarts the mission.
    • Radio voices (app 0.6.2): the flight screen decides when a pilot speaks — wing orders and status requests (acknowledge, status), a wingman lost (down, said by another), chatter clips (MissionRun::voices) and the computer voice when the result is decided (mission_accomplished / mission_failed). See Audio.

    • Flight session (flight.rs): see the frame loop on the overview. Views F1–F8; after death the camera orbits the wreck; Esc opens a menu (Esc resume, R restart, K controls, L launcher; it points to ? for the keys instead of listing them); P pauses; ? (or Shift+F1) opens the keys overlay (draw_help, help_open), which pauses the battle and lists every action by group with its current keys, in as many columns as the screen needs. The player's cockpit may have 2D art, a 3D model, or both; the HUD uses the cockpit's design space in the cockpit view and the default glass layout in the other views. A 3D cockpit on a window narrower than its design widens the field of view (cockpit_fov), and its anchored instruments are drawn on the dashboard in perspective (draw_anchored). Stage 2 additions:

      • Cockpit layout editor (F12 in the cockpit view; the battle pauses): LayoutEditor holds the selected instrument, the drag in progress and a status message; edit_layout handles Tab, 2D drags and nudges, 3D ray picks on the cockpit model's top LOD, resizing by wheel or +/-, and S, which save_layout writes as an addon <user data>/mods/<campaign>-cockpit-<id>/ (pack.yaml + rules/cockpit-layout.yaml). See Cockpits.
      • Wingman comms menu (], pad LB + left stick click): the six WING_MENU entries (fire at will, attack my target, ignore my target, evasive, retreat, status report), chosen with digits 1–6 or the d-pad and A; B or ] closes it. While open, the d-pad and A belong to the menu, not to throttle, MFD and afterburner. A status report puts one line per wingman on the message line.
      • Messages (event_messages): system damage and repair ("… DAMAGED: HALF EFFICIENCY", "… INOPERATIVE: REPAIRS UNDERWAY", "… BACK ON-LINE", "… REPAIRED", "… DESTROYED"), "ENGINES AND THRUSTERS OUT: SHIP DISABLED", "TARGET DISABLED", "… JUMPED OUT", "… CAPTURED", "HYPERJUMP COMPLETE". L repeats the last message.
      • Jumped-out end screen: "JUMPED TO HYPERSPACE", with the captured prizes listed; Enter flies again, Esc returns to the menu.

      Stage 3 interlude additions:

      • Tablet console (C1, app 0.4.0): when the console is on (--console, console.enabled), app::start_console starts a remote::server::ConsoleServer into AppContext::console (a taken port is reported and the game carries on without it). Each frame FlightSession::console_frame merges the console's input into RawInput, sends welcome and the catalogue (again when the player's craft class changes) and 20 Hz telemetry. Until a console pairs, and while the menu is open, the bottom-left corner shows CONSOLE <url> CODE <code>. See Remote console.

    The simulator (app 0.6.3)¶

    app::simulator (Stage 3.9, R-MSN-010): a setup screen in front of any mission of the loaded campaign. Rows, chosen with Up/Down and changed with Left/Right; Enter flies; Esc returns to the launcher:

    Row Values Effect on the copy (simulator::apply)
    Mission every mission, in launcher order The mission to copy. Its briefing is shown below the rows.
    Ship the playable ships The player's craft; "(the mission's own)" when the mission names player.class.
    Environment the mission's own (or open space), then every environment environment replaced.
    Asteroids off / on 40 objects of the campaign's asteroid class in a 4.5 km sphere 4.5 km ahead, spin 6 °/s.
    Minefield off / on 20 mines of the campaign's mine class in a 2.5 km sphere 4.5 km ahead.
    Black hole off / on A black hole 9 km ahead and 800 m below, pull 12, radius 450 m.
    Ion storm off / on Radar at 40 %.
    Enemy force ×0.5, ×1, ×1.5, ×2 Every enemy role's count range and every wave of an enemy role scaled and rounded, never below 1. Friendly and objective roles are unchanged.
    Fly — Starts the copy.
    • Hazard classes come from the campaign itself (simulator::hazard_classes): the asteroid class is the first object class whose id contains asteroid, the mine class the first of kind: mine. A campaign without one shows "(none in this campaign)" on that row and the toggle does nothing — the simulator never assumes a class a mod may not have.
    • Refusal: simulator::ship_allowed refuses a ship with atmospheric: false in an environment with physics: atmosphere; the reason is shown under the rows and nothing starts.
    • Flying: Fly returns Next::Simulate { mission, ship } with the modified MissionDef (boxed: it is large); the app starts it with FlightSession::start_custom, which keeps the copy in FlightSession::custom so that "again" from the debrief (or R in the pause menu) returns Next::Simulate with the same setup instead of the original mission.
    • The difficulty level is the launcher's (D); the mission's own values per level apply before the force multiplier.
    • With the defaults (own environment, no hazards, ×1) the copy equals the mission (tested).
    • SimSetup is plain data and apply and ship_allowed are pure, so the logic is unit-tested without a window; the drawing is a manual test (MT-3-51 – MT-3-54).

    The strategy screen (app 0.7.1)¶

    app::strategy::StrategyScreen (Stage 4.10) shows a campaign::Campaign. Since app 0.16.0 (main ac7ae62) it is five widgets placed by the screens.starmap layout: the top bar (date, funds, score, base switcher, the clock's buttons — SPEEDS: paused, 1, 6, 24 and 120 campaign hours per real second — Save, Menu), the star map (zoom, pan, pick, hover, legend), the info panel for the selection (Sel) with its actions (up to six since app 0.16.1), the list buttons (Tab: eleven since app 0.16.8 with Reports, twelve with the testbed's Debug; the Supply list, Tab::Logistics, since app 0.9.0) and the list panel whose rows carry their actions as buttons; notices, interceptions (with Auto-resolve since app 0.16.12), convoy ambushes, construction strikes (app 0.16.4), choices, offers, title cards and the save-slot choice (app 0.16.14) are modal prompts, and the ending has a screen of its own (app 0.16.14). How a frame runs: Presentation › Widgets and screen layouts. It turns rows into orders on the campaign and draws; the rules live in campaign (Campaign). The launcher leads with Entry::ContinueCampaign and Entry::NewCampaign when rules.strategy is set and starts selected on them; --strategy opens a new campaign. A campaign battle sets FlightSession::campaign_battle: its debrief returns Next::Strategy (Enter or Esc) with AppContext::battle_report, the pause menu's L/Q is a retreat and R (restart) is disabled. Next::CampaignBattle carries the first craft's fitted loadout, applied with flight::apply_loadout (app 0.7.2). Testbed cheats (app 0.8.1, plan Testbed adds of Stage 4): Ctrl+M payday, Ctrl+K knowitall, Ctrl+B quickbase — the original's own cheat list — and Ctrl+J jump to battle from the contact selected on the map or in the Contacts list, each a Campaign::cheat_* method (Campaign › Testbed cheats). Headless, --strategy-soak <days> plays a campaign's strategy layer with campaign::autoplay (app 0.8.0), and --balance (app 0.15.0, src/balance.rs) prints every class's protection, sustained beam damage and the seconds of perfect fire each playable ship needs to destroy it — effective_hp, sustained_dps and time_to_kill are pure and tested (balance::tests::energy_limits_sustained_fire_and_kill_times_follow). Player's guide: Campaign.

    Stage 5 testbed tools (app 0.16.13, #47, R-TST-010): Ctrl+D toggles StrategyScreen::debug, which adds Tab::Debug to the list buttons and to Tab's cycle (it is filtered out otherwise). Its rows are each arc's step and every story event with story.fired[e] and Campaign::holds on its when, each with the action Act::FireEvent(e); Campaign::fire_event returns the events it raised and the screen turns them into its usual prompts (a notice, a choice, an offer, a card, the ending). Start at a chapter: --strategy --chapter N (Options::chapter) makes app::run play the new campaign forward with campaign::autoplay_to_chapter(def, c, N, 1500.0) — repeated five-day autoplay_from steps until the first arc's arc_step reaches N or 1500 days pass — and open StrategyScreen::from_campaign.

    The condition console (app 0.16.5, #20, R-TST-009) lives in FlightSession: cond: Option<String> is the text typed so far; backquote (KeyCode::GraveAccent, hard-wired, not a binding) opens and closes it, Esc closes it. While it is open the pause-menu and help handling is skipped, characters from get_char_pressed are appended (the backquote and control characters dropped), and the simulation does not tick. Each frame the text is evaluated with MissionRun::evaluate (Conditions) and drawn in a panel at the bottom: = value in green, an error in orange.

    Debugging¶

    • openinterceptor --data <cd> --scenario flight-test --ship firestar --view chase jumps straight into a scenario; add --campaign default (or interceptor) to pick the campaign.
    • openinterceptor --no-original --scenario rocket-vs-saucer --ship comet --screenshot shot.png saves the cockpit view after 120 frames. The window opens at 1280×960 (4:3), so this shows the widened 3D-cockpit field of view; compare with a manual run in a 16:9 window (MT-2-14).
    • Scenario errors (unknown class, faction or leader) are shown in orange on the launcher and printed to standard error.
    • openinterceptor --campaign default --soak rocket-vs-saucer --ship comet --difficulty cadet plays the scenario headless and prints the player's fate (src/soak.rs): how long a passive ship survives, or whether an AI-flown one wins (--soak-pilot ai), per difficulty level. --soak-pilot idle leaves the player's craft sitting still: in a defence mission it measures how long the defended object holds on its own (SoakPilot::Idle).
    • A layout saved by F12 that seems ignored: the addon targets the campaign that was loaded when it was saved; check --list shows <campaign>-cockpit-<id> and that it is not disabled.
    • A simulator row that does nothing: the campaign has no asteroid class (no id containing asteroid) or no kind: mine class; the row says "(none in this campaign)". A Fly that does nothing: read the orange line under the rows (a ship that cannot enter an atmosphere).
    • openinterceptor --mission defend_station --ship comet starts a mission directly; --soak defend_station --soak-pilot ai --seconds 400 plays it headless and prints the result.
    • In any mission battle press ` to type a condition and see its value (app 0.16.5); the battle waits.
    • openinterceptor --no-original --strategy --chapter 6 opens the default campaign at chapter 6; Ctrl+D on the strategy screen shows the story's state and fires events (app 0.16.13).
