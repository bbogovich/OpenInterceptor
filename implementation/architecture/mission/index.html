    Missions¶

    src/content/mission.rs (definitions) and src/mission/ (the runtime): battles described by roles, waves, objectives and outcomes, refereed on top of the simulation. The battle conditions are src/script/battle.rs, the outcome actions src/script/action.rs. Plan: Stage 3 part 1 (app 0.3.0). The modder's view is Missions.

    Architecture Overview¶

    flowchart TD
    Y["missions/*.yaml<br/>(any rules folder)"] --> RB["rules::build_at<br/>validate (files::mission), inherit, variants, per_level"]
    RB --> MP["MissionDef::parse(id, Get, pack, classes, levels)<br/>roles, waves, objectives, optional, timer,<br/>outcomes, chatter, debrief"]
    BS["script::battle::schema(role names, levels)"] --> MP
    AC["script::action::MISSION_ACTIONS"] --> MP
    MP --> TX["Content: @keys resolved<br/>(name, briefing, debrief, chatter, message args)"]
    TX --> RU["Ruleset::missions (sorted by order)"]
    RU --> ST["MissionRun::start(def, rules, shapes, player class)<br/>player + roles arriving at the start → World"]
    ST --> W["World"]

    Layering. mission sits between sim and app: it reads and spawns into a World (through World::spawn_placement, the same path scenario objects take), but the simulation knows nothing about missions, so sim stays testable on its own.

    One frame of a mission¶

    flowchart LR
    FS["FlightSession::frame"] --> STEP["World::step() × n ticks"]
    STEP --> UP["MissionRun::update(&mut World)<br/>after every tick"]
    UP --> L1["1. losses per role from member state<br/>(destroyed, departed, captured events)"]
    L1 --> L2["2. arrive: conditions → spawn_role;<br/>waves (once); chatter (once) → message lines;<br/>optional objectives latched"]
    L2 --> L3["3. end: retreat (player departed) →<br/>killed (player destroyed) →<br/>lose → win → draw → timer"]
    L3 -->|result| DB["Debrief: result, text, optional (met?),<br/>campaign actions, kills, losses, seconds;<br/>battle actions (message) run"]
    DB --> OV["debrief overlay over the frozen battle<br/>Enter: again · Esc/L: launcher"]
    • Placement (spawn_role): the count is rolled from [min, max], each member's class from the role's list; the arrival point is distance along bearing (yaw, pitch; [0, 0] = −Z from the player's start; random within ±20° pitch when absent) from the player's start or the centre of the from role's survivors; members stand side by side spread apart across the line of sight and face the player's start. Placed members get the role's faction, AI role, behaviour, protect flag, state, hull, destroyed systems and speed.
    • Counting: a member that has left the world is departed (hyperspace) if its slot still holds it (World::same_slot) and it departed, otherwise destroyed; captures come from Captured events. Kills are hostile losses, losses the player's own faction's.
    • Determinism: the mission's random choices come from its own generator seeded by starfield_seed, and condition random(p) from the same stream, so a mission replays identically with the same inputs.
    • Conditions are evaluated through BattleCx, which implements the script language's Context for this battle; role names are constants of the battle schema, compiled to indices.

    The killed result (app 0.3.1)¶

    When the player's craft is destroyed — its slot no longer holds it (!World::same_slot, the slot may have been reused) or it is not alive — the result is killed, checked before the mission's own conditions. Before 0.3.1 a mission whose lose condition included not player_alive reported the player's death as a loss, and the debrief showed the lose text, which usually describes the objective failing ("the base was destroyed"). Measured with the idle soak pilot, that was what players of xcom_base_defense saw on veteran: the base was fine, the player had been killed in 15–18 s.

    killed takes the mission's own outcomes: {killed: …} if it has any, else its lose outcomes; the debrief text is debrief: {killed: …} if given, else empty. The debrief panel's headline is "YOUR CRAFT WAS DESTROYED".

    Launcher, --mission, soak¶

    The launcher lists missions after the scenarios (Mission: name, the briefing as the description, the ship choice when player.class is choose) and scrolls the list to keep the selection near the middle (launcher::Menu::first_visible, "^ more" / "v more" markers). Selecting one returns Next::Mission { mission, ship }; --mission <id> starts one directly. soak::run_mission plays a mission headless with the passive, AI or idle pilot and reports the result (--soak <mission id>). A unit test holds the balance promise of R-MSN-011: in the default campaign at cadet, defend_station with the idle pilot is still running after 150 s.

    AI changes made with missions¶

    Enemies now launch missiles only when closing in (within 2.5 × their attack range, at least 2 km); the first launch comes 3 s plus a random part of one interval into the attack, and each interval varies by ±25 %. Every AI pilot's random sequence is seeded per ship in World::spawn, so pilots that start together do not act together.

    Key Symbols¶

    Symbol What it is
    content::mission::MissionDef A parsed mission: id, name, briefing, order, player_class, player_position, roles, waves, win/lose/draw, optional, timer, outcomes, chatter, debrief, starfield_seed.
    RoleDef, Arrival, WaveDef, ChatterDef A role (classes, count range, faction, AI, behaviour, protect, state, hull, destroyed systems, speed, at, arrive); an arrival (distance, bearing, from, spread); a wave; a radio line.
    mission::MissionRun The running mission: its definition, the player's id, per-role RoleRuns, wave/chatter/optional latches, its generator, kills, losses, debrief.
    mission::RoleRun members, arrived, destroyed, retreated, captured.
    mission::Debrief result (win, lose, draw, retreat, killed), text, optional: Vec<(name, met)>, campaign actions (as text), kills, losses, seconds.
    mission::BattleCx The battle Context for conditions.
    script::battle::QUERIES, schema(roles, levels) The battle context's queries.
    script::action::{ActionDef, Action, Scope, MISSION_ACTIONS} Outcome actions with Battle or Campaign scope.
    schema::Ty::Either A schema type with two accepted forms (count, bearing, class).
    World::spawn_placement, World::same_slot Spawn one placed object; whether an id's slot still holds its ship.
    app::Next::Mission, FlightSession::start_mission Starting a mission from the launcher or --mission.

    API Reference¶

    • MissionDef::parse(id, Get, pack, classes, levels) -> Result<MissionDef, String>; def.outcome(result) -> &[Action].
    • MissionRun::start(&MissionDef, Arc<Ruleset>, Arc<Vec<Shape>>, Option<ShipClassId>) -> Result<(World, MissionRun), String>; run.update(&mut World) -> Vec<String> (message lines); run.spawn_role(&mut World, role, count, at) -> Result<usize, String>.
    • script::battle::schema(&[String], &[String]) -> Schema; script::action::parse(text, MISSION_ACTIONS) -> Result<Action, String>; action.display().
    • soak::run_mission(&Content, id, ship, seconds, pilot).

    Debugging¶

    • --check reports every mission problem with file, line and path (missions.x: roles.y…, unknown roles in conditions, unknown actions).
    • --mission <id> --ship <id> jumps straight in; --soak <id> --difficulty <level> [--soak-pilot ai|idle] plays it headless and prints soak mission <id> (<level>, <pilot>): <result> after <n> s; <k> kills — the quickest way to check that a mission can be both won and lost.
    • A role that never appears: its arrive: condition never held, or its at.from role has no survivors (it then appears at the player's start). arrived(role) in a chatter line makes arrivals visible in play.
    • A "MISSION ERROR: …" message line means a role could not be spawned (an unknown slot in a station's modules, for example).
