    Stage 2 — Combat, objects and behaviours¶

    Version 0.3.0. Status: complete (app 0.3.0, main d7553f5, 2026-11-11), except the deferred items below — XSPT sprite explosions and the model-set extras — and the manual passes, which are still to be recorded. Stage 3 part 1 (missions) ships in the same build. Every craft, station and weapon of both campaigns fights correctly, looks right in every state, and is driven by data: model sets, debris, behaviours with difficulty variants. The mod structure (campaign/addon/library, parent) lands here because every later stage loads content through it.

    Design: mod structure, objects and models, behaviours.
