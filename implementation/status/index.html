    Stage 0 results¶

    • All 297 XMSH meshes in xobjects1.dll and xobjects2.dll parse, with 4 LODs each.
    • The three player hulls and an alien saucer were rendered with their original textures by an independent prototype decoder, confirming the mesh, UV and texture layouts.
    • XTXR textures (all sizes), cockpit TGAs (colour key), ADPCM sound effects and PCM music loops are understood. Balance data is plain INI.
    • Unknowns that do not block stages 1–2: XSPT RLE details, a few mesh fields, the MFD animation file mfdanim.mps, the AIFF-C codec of the extracted CD audio.
