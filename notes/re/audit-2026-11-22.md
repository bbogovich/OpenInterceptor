# Audit 2026-11-22: the `interceptor` campaign against the original

This audit compares the engine (`main` at 2bc263b, 0.22.0; release binary built 2026-11-22 00:08) with what
the original game does. The sources are `notes/re/flight/README.md` (cited as **F§n**),
`notes/re/ini-files.md` (**INI§n**), `notes/re/wrappers/navigation.md` (**NAV**) and facts read from the
player's own `GameSettings.ini`. Facts only: no original text, art or files are reproduced. Screenshots
were taken to a scratch folder and are not in the repository.

Severity: **blocks play** (progress or a core mechanic is broken), **wrong feel** (playable, but the numbers
or behaviour differ from the original), **cosmetic** (presentation only).

Runs made:

- `--check` passes for both campaigns (interceptor: 38 classes, 0 warnings, 0 errors; default: 0/0).
- `--balance`.
- `--soak` on 9 scenarios with the `ai`, `passive` and `idle` pilots.
- `--strategy-soak` for 1, 30, 90, 180 and 365 days.
- A screenshot of each of the 9 screens in `rules/screens.yaml`.
- `default` campaign: 4 scenario soaks and a 180-day strategy soak.

There were no crashes or panics.

## A. Blocks play

### D-01 The research tech-id table is off by one, so research gates are scrambled

- **Area:** strategy, research and availability.
- **Original:** `GameSettings.ini` lists the tech ids in its own comment block, from 00 ACCUMULATOR_SHIELDS
  to 100, with 21, 32 and 41 as placeholders. With that list every item's `TechRequired` names its own
  topic: GatlingLaser 34 is gatling lasers, HeavyLaser 37 heavy lasers, EngineType2 36 gravity-induction
  drive type 2, ArmorType2 68 tri-titanium armour type 2, ShieldType3 67 trans-dimensional shields type 3,
  and so on for all 60 items checked.
- **Engine:** `packs/interceptor/strategy/references.yaml:6-74` (`tech_ids`, "from the exe table";
  vault: confidence derived) is shifted by one against that list. Under it, Gatling needs *fusion
  missile*, Heavy Laser *gravity induction drive*, Fusion Missile (33) maps to nothing, ShieldType2 needs
  *ECM*, and every R_ prerequisite moves the same way (`src/content/strategy.rs:650-666`).
- **Effect:**
  - With every "special" (127) prerequisite met, 55 of 71 topics are reachable, against 71 of 71 with
    the INI list. Without salvage it is 5 against 9.
  - The 365-day soak stalls at 4/71 topics from day 90 on.
- **Fix:** pack data. Regenerate `tech_ids` from the INI's id list, or correct the exe-table reading
  (`tools/interceptor_strategy.py`). Record the decision in the vault.
- **Verify:** a test that maps every item's `TechRequired` to the topic of the same name.
  `--strategy-soak 365` should then pass 4 topics.

### D-02 Hull repair is missing, and system repair uses the wrong rate and rules

- **Area:** combat, repair.
- **Original (F§4.3, verified):**
  - Once a second, 2 points (4 with a ShipDamageControlComputer) go to the first item in this order
    that needs them: engine, **hull**, shield generator, flight control, hyperdrive, sensor, mounts.
  - A system at HP 0 is never repaired; flight control is set to 1 instead.
  - A Tachyon hit slows repair to once every 10 s.
- **Engine:**
  - `src/sim/systems.rs:378-402` repairs systems only, never the hull (no `hull +=` anywhere in `src/sim`).
  - It picks the system in the worst condition instead of following the fixed order.
  - The DCC's `repair_rate: 4` (`rules/systems.yaml:67`) is used as a **multiplier**, so the rate is
    2 × 4 = 8 per s instead of 4.
  - A system at 0 HP is repaired back to service (`SystemState::ONLINE_AT`, `src/sim/mod.rs:261`). It
    is lost only below −50 % (`destroy_below: 0.5 # DERIVED`, `systems.yaml:98`).
  - `systems.yaml:99` still labels `repair_rate: 2` as DERIVED, though F§4.3 verifies it.
- **Severity:** wrong feel, close to blocking. A Lightning has 100 hull points, and 2 per second of
  repair roughly doubles how long it survives under light fire. This is part of why a passive Lightning
  dies in about 10 s (D-42).
- **Fix:** code and data.
  - Repair hull first after the engine, using `damage.repair_order` or a fixed original order.
  - Make the DCC an absolute rate of 4 (`repair_rate: 2` as an additive bonus, or a new `repair_with`).
  - Make HP 0 final (`destroy_below: 0`).
- **Verify:** unit test: a ship at 50/100 hull regains 2/s, or 4/s with a DCC. Soak `duel-pirate-lightning`
  with `--soak-pilot ai` and compare survival time.

## B. Wrong feel: combat and damage

### D-03 Accumulator shields (ShieldType4) are not modelled

- **Original (F§4):** while the struck bank is up, an ordinary hit does no damage, and a quarter of it is
  added to the shield.
- **Engine:**
  - `apply_hit` (`src/sim/combat.rs:417-500`) has no accumulator case; no code mentions it.
  - The Shade and Harbinger fit `ShieldType4` (`ships.yaml`, the `shade` and `harbinger` entries), so
    they behave as an ordinary 350-point shield.
- **Effect:** `duel-shade` (ai pilot): the Shade dies without hurting the player.
- **Fix:** code (a shield `accumulates` flag on the equipment or class) plus pack data on ShieldType4.
- **Verify:** unit test: a beam hit on a raised accumulator bank raises the bank by damage/4. Then re-soak
  `duel-shade`.

### D-04 The Tachyon Pulser hits hull and armour like any beam

- **Original (F§4.4):**
  - The struck shield absorbs 1:1 (skipped while cloaked).
  - The rest bypasses armour and hull and goes only to the second system table (engine 50 %, hyperdrive
    10 %, …).
  - It also sets the 10 s slow-repair flag.
- **Engine:** `move_bolts` → `apply_hit(.., DamageMode::Normal, ..)` (`combat.rs:350-358`). Tachyon is
  ordinary beam damage.
- **Fix:** code. Add a beam `damage:` mode (`systems_only`) and use it for `tachyon_pulser` in
  `weapons.yaml`.
- **Verify:** unit test: a Tachyon hit never lowers hull or armour. Soak `duel-phantasm`.

### D-05 System damage: the wrong table and no weapon damage

- **Original (F§4.1):**
  - Up to 5 rolls on a weighted table: 40 % beam mounts or launchers, 10 % shield generator, 10 % flight
    control (set to 1), 10 % sensor, 10 % engine, …
  - Shield, sensor and engine damage carries over to the next roll.
  - Mounts at 25–50 % fire at half rate, and at ≤25 % they cannot fire (F§3).
  - Launchers at ≤25 % cannot fire, and their rate halves at ≤50 % (F§5.2).
- **Engine:**
  - `damage_system` (`systems.rs:406-440`) hits one uniformly random fitted system.
  - Beam mounts and launchers have no hit points at all.
- **Fix:** code. Make mounts and launchers damageable, add a weighted `system_table:` in `damage:`, and
  add the carry-over.
- **Verify:** a statistical unit test over 10,000 hits. In the testbed, a damaged mount fires at half rate.

### D-06 System condition thresholds differ

- **Original:**

  | System | Above 50 % | Below 50 % | Below 25 % | Below 15 % |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Engine (F§5) | full | × 0.5 | × 0.25 | no thrust |
  | Shield generator recharge (F§3) | full | half | none | none |

  Flight control works as `q = HP/40`, continuously.
- **Engine:** `Condition` (`src/sim/mod.rs:218-238`): Ok 1.0, Damaged (≤50 %) 0.5, and only HP ≤ 0 gives
  0. No 25 %/15 % tiers, and flight control is stepped.
- **Fix:** code. Make efficiency a per-kind table in data, for example
  `damage.efficiency: {engines: [[50%,0.5],[25%,0.25],[15%,0]]}`.
- **Verify:** unit tests per kind.

### D-07 Permeator and Enveloping split their damage wrongly

- **Original (F§5.2):**
  - Permeator: half the damage runs the normal chain on the struck side (shield → armour → hull); the
    other half skips the shield and goes armour → hull.
  - Enveloping: half runs the **fore** chain (shield and armour) and half the **aft** chain.
  - Both always injure the pilot by 1–3.
- **Engine:**
  - `DamageMode::BypassShields` skips the shields for the whole hit.
  - `Enveloping` splits only the shields; the armour comes from the struck side alone
    (`combat.rs:441-475`).
  - `area_blast` (mines) also uses `Enveloping`.
- **Fix:** code.
- **Verify:** unit tests with known bank values.

### D-08 Pilot injury is not modelled

- **Original (F§4.2):** a hull hit rolls a `min(damage,100)` % chance of 1–3 pilot health. At 0 the pilot
  is unconscious; below 0 the pilot is dead and the ship becomes a tumbling wreck. Collisions cost 5–10.
- **Engine:** none (`systems.yaml:91` says "not modelled yet").
- **Fix:** code, with the strategy pilot health carried into flight.
- **Verify:** unit test, then a campaign battle in which a pilot is injured.

### D-09 Automatic point defence that the original does not have, and player beams cannot hit missiles

- **Original (F§4.4):** there is no automatic PD turret. A beam bolt that strikes a missile has a 90 %
  chance to apply its `Damage`. A missile has HP clamp(HitPoints, 10, 500) and dies when the damage
  exceeds it.
- **Engine:**
  - `xcom_freighter` and `patton` carry `point_defence: {range: 900m, rate: 3, chance: 35%}`
    (`ships.yaml:291-292`, `308-309`), handled in `combat.rs:67-104`.
  - `first_hit` (`combat.rs:309`) tests ships only, so bolts pass through missiles.
- **Fix:** pack data (remove the turrets from the interceptor classes) and code (bolt-vs-missile hits;
  use missile `hit_points`).
- **Verify:** unit test: a laser bolt through an XWinder destroys it 9 times in 10.

### D-10 Cloak: no energy drain, shields still work, missiles fire from cloak

- **Original (F§3, F§4, F§5.2):**
  - Cloaked, the charge falls by 50 per second **instead of** shield recharge, and the cloak drops at 0.
  - Shields are skipped when a cloaked ship is hit.
  - A cloaked ship cannot fire missiles (LockTime 0).
- **Engine:**
  - The cloak only costs 6 power steps (`systems.yaml` `cloak.power: 6`).
  - `recharge` (`systems.rs:309`) keeps recharging the shields.
  - `apply_hit` uses the shields regardless.
  - `handle_missile_controls` fires and drops the cloak (`combat.rs:256-298`).
- **Fix:** code (a cloak energy drain and the shield and missile rules) plus data (`drain: 50` on the
  cloak).
- **Verify:** unit tests.

### D-11 Energy does not recharge while the afterburner is on

- **Original:** recharge runs once a second regardless (F§3); the afterburner costs 100 per second (F§3).
- **Engine:** `if !ship.afterburning` in `recharge` (`systems.rs:311`), so the net drain is 100/s instead
  of 100 − recharge.
- **Fix:** code (drop the condition, or make it a flag).
- **Verify:** unit test.

### D-12 Missile flight and fuse differ

- **Original (F§5.2):**
  - Off target, the throttle falls by up to 0.1 per frame (e > 15°), so missiles slow in hard turns.
  - Commanded acceleration is `max(throttle×Speed, 250)`.
  - The fuse also fires within 25 m when the error exceeds 45°.
  - The blast does `ImpactDamage` to **every** object within 15 m and `ProximityDamage` to every object
    within 15–200 m, with no falloff.
  - An expired missile is a dud and vanishes without a blast.
  - A lock is lost above 170° of error.
- **Engine (`src/sim/guidance.rs:200-268`):**
  - The missile reaches `Speed` in 1 s and never slows.
  - Proximity damage goes only to the target, inside `proximity_radius` 25 m.
  - Expiry pushes a `MissileExploded` effect.
  - No 170° loss.
- **Severity:** wrong feel (missiles are harder to dodge; no splash on wingmen).
- **Fix:** code.
- **Verify:** guidance unit tests; testbed `combat-test`.

### D-13 Missile launcher fire rate and rounds

- **Launcher fire rate.**
  - Original (F§5.2): the next shot is `1000/RateOfFire` ms (shots per second), and the next mount of
    the same type is ready after `500/RateOfFire`. XWinder launcher RoF 4, Fusion 6, Elerium 1.
  - Engine: a fixed `MISSILE_REFIRE = 0.6` s for everything (`combat.rs:32`, `:278`).
- **Rounds per launcher.**
  - Original `*Launcher Rounds`: XWinder 5, Permeator 3, Sidestep 4, Fusion 2, Elerium 4, Enveloping 3,
    Parallax 3, PassiveCloaking 2, Doppelganger 1, Nova 1.
  - Engine `strategy/campaign.yaml:61-71` `items:` (DERIVED): 4/4/4/3/2/2/2/2/3/1. Seven of the ten
    differ.
- **Fix:**
  - Pack data: inherit `rounds` from `original:GameSettings.ini/<X>Launcher` (F§5.2 confirms `Rounds`
    fills the mount).
  - Code: a per-launcher `rate_of_fire`.
- **Verify:** check an equipped craft's racks; unit-test the refire.

### D-14 Beam link modes differ

- **Original (F§3):** mode 2 fires every mount, mode 1 every mount of the *selected type*, and mode 0 the
  selected mount.
- **Engine:** `LinkMode::Linked` fires the *next two consecutive mounts* (`combat.rs:182-190`). A whole
  group also waits for its slowest mount and for the energy of the whole group.
- **Severity:** wrong feel for mixed fits (the Firestar, Avenger).
- **Fix:** code.
- **Verify:** unit test with a mixed fit.

### D-15 Ships spawn at throttle 0

- **Original (F§2):** the power block starts at throttle 50 %.
- **Engine:** `throttle: 0.0` in `World::spawn` (`src/sim/mod.rs:762`).
- **Fix:** data (`flight.start_throttle`) or code.
- **Verify:** the testbed start.

### D-16 Station hulls sum HP1–HP4; the original uses only HP1

- **Original (F§1, verified):** `0x41f9fe` returns **HP1** as the station's maximum HP. HP2–HP4 and SD have
  no code references.
- **Engine:** `rules.rs:2278-2287` sums HP1..HP4, and `stations.yaml` marks it "(DERIVED)". So `xcom_base`
  has 11,500 instead of 6,000, `alien_base` 7,700 instead of 5,000, `xcom_outpost` 7,200 instead of 3,500,
  and `alien_outpost` 6,450 instead of 3,500.
- **Fix:** code (read `hp1` only) and pack comments.
- **Verify:** `--balance` hull column; base-assault soak.

### D-17 Pirate and corporate hulls copy the X-COM hulls

- **Original:** `Ship_PirateType1..3` and `Ship_CorporateType1..3` have their own values (INI§1.9):

  | Hull | HP (engine) | Beam mounts | Missile mounts | Special mounts |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Pirate1 | 150 (100) | 2 | 0 | 1 |
  | Pirate2 | 250 | 4 | 3 | 1 |
  | Pirate3 | 400 | 4 | 2 | 2 |
  | Corp1 | 125 (100) | 2 | 2 | 2 |
  | Corp2 | 200 (250) | 2 | 0 | 1 |
  | Corp3 | 450 (400) | 2 | 0 | 1 |

- **Engine:** `ships.yaml:232-290` inherit `lightning`, `firestar` and `avenger`, so they get the X-COM HP,
  beams and missiles (for example, a Corporate Firestar with 4 beams and 3 missile types; a Pirate
  Lightning with missiles).
- **Fix:** pack data. Inherit `original:GameSettings.ini/Ship_PirateTypeN` and `Ship_CorporateTypeN`, then
  set flight values, `mass`, `ai_speed` and `ai_turn` (the table rows already exist), and loadouts within
  the mount counts.
- **Verify:** `--balance`.

### D-18 Patton protection is set directly instead of scaled from its equipment

- **Original (F§3):** the Patton has shields × 3 and armour × 4 of its fitted equipment.
- **Engine:** `ships.yaml:308-316` sets `shields: 1200`, `armor: 1500 # DERIVED`, with no
  `protection_scale` and no `fitted`.
- **Severity:** wrong feel (minor).
- **Fix:** pack data (`fitted` + `protection_scale: {shields: 3, armor: 4}`) once its fit is known.
- **Verify:** `--balance`.

## C. Wrong feel: AI

### D-19 AI speed requests in m/s are read as a fraction of the pack's `max_speed`

- **Original (F§5.1):** throttle is a fraction (−0.25…1). `SetSpeed(v)` sets throttle = v/S.
- **Engine:** `driven` (`src/sim/flight.rs:257`) turns `hold_speed` into `v / class.max_speed`, so the
  terminal speed is `S·v / (2·max_speed)`. Behaviours that pass a fraction × max_speed are right. Those
  that pass a **real speed** come out wrong:
  - formation slots (`ai.rs:633-635`: the leader's speed + gap);
  - the escort/trail code (`ai.rs:781`);
  - tow (`ai.rs:943`).

  These come out at 0.42 × (Lightning AI, S 500 / max 600) or about 0.33 × (Wraith) of the wanted speed.
  Wingmen cannot hold the player's 300 m/s.
- **Fix:** code. In `driven`, map a speed in m/s to the throttle `v·drag/S`, clamped to [−0.25, 1], or
  separate "speed fraction" from "speed in m/s" in `Controls`.
- **Verify:** unit test: an AI Lightning with `hold_speed: 200` settles at 200 ± 5 m/s. Then the testbed
  `escort` and `mixed-dogfight` with wingmen.

### D-20 The AI burns its afterburner energy for no thrust

- **Original:** AI agents never run the afterburner routine (F§5.1: no MaxSpeed, no afterburner).
- **Engine:**
  - `fly_to_slot` (`ai.rs:622`, `:636`), hazard avoidance (`ai.rs:695`) and `retreat` (`ai.rs:1026`) set
    `c.afterburner`.
  - `flight::step` (`flight.rs:117-121`) still charges 100/s, and `recharge` stops (D-11).
  - Yet `driven` ignores the boost for AI ships.
- **Effect:** a fleeing or catching-up AI empties its pool and cannot fire.
- **Fix:** code (no afterburner for `Pilot::Ai` under `flight.drive`).
- **Verify:** unit test: an AI's energy is unchanged after `retreat` for 5 s.

### D-21 The AI steering law is much softer than the original's

- **Original (F§5):** the per-axis rate is `clamp(error°, ±max)`, used as rad/s, which closes a small
  error within about one frame. The sluggish mode uses `error° × 0.06`, unclamped.
- **Engine:** `steer_towards` (`ai.rs:182-197`) gives a stick of `clamp(4·sin(err))` × `ai_turn`, a time
  constant of about 0.12 s for small errors (about 7× softer). `SLUGGISH` (0.06) multiplies that softer
  law.
- **Effect:** worse aim tracking, which changes time-to-kill and the effect of `accuracy`.
- **Fix:** code. Add an `original` steering law (behaviour `skill.steer: original`).
- **Verify:** unit test: an error of 1° is closed within 2 ticks. Compare duel hit rates before and after.

### D-22 The AI fires missiles only after a full sensor lock

- **Original (F§5.1 step 6):**
  - The AI's own timer starts when both errors are within ±5°.
  - Missiles may fire while `now < start + LockTime`, with `chance(5+2d)` per frame, `dist < R/2`,
    and ≥ 5 s apart.
- **Engine:**
  - `attack` requires `ship.lock.locked` (`ai.rs:892`): the player's 2.5° cone held for 2.5 s.
  - The interval is 5 s × 0.75–1.25, so it can be 3.75 s.
- **Effect:** the AI launches far fewer missiles, and the minimum spacing is wrong.
- **Fix:** code.
- **Verify:** count `MissileLaunched` events per minute in a `duel-wraith` soak.

### D-23 Smaller AI gaps

These come from F§5.1:

- **Sprint:** no sprint above 3 km. The original's is `a = 1.0625 S` when `temperament ≠ 0` or d > 2.
- **Break-off:** at 120 + 0.15·max_speed m (`ai.rs:900`), where the original's is 250 m.
- **Weapon choice:** the `0x5853b8` routine is missing. The engine always aims with the first beam's
  speed (`ai.rs:854`).
- **Damaged controls:** the wobble for `q < 1` is absent (F§5).

All wrong feel (minor), all code fixes.

## E. Strategy screens: text mis-sized or not aligned with its control (from screenshots)

All `list` widgets go through one generic routine, `Strategy::draw_panel`
(`src/app/strategy.rs:2046-2113`). Its sizes are fixed whatever the widget's rect and the art:

- 22·k padding, a 20·k title at `r[1]+34k`, 21·k rows and 15·k text;
- a 3-line detail block plus a 26·k button row reserved at the bottom (`detail_h = 3·line + 34k`).

`value` widgets use `place_text` (`strategy.rs:1783-1790`): a baseline at `r[1] + r[3]/2 + 0.35·size`.
Star labels come from `draw_map` (`strategy.rs:2165`). Positions are in the 640×480 design space.

- **D-32 `equip_ships.ships` (rect 17,397,500×60):** the rect is shorter than the reserved detail and
  button block (≈ 97 + 26 + 22 design px).
  - The detail line ("LIGHTNING-1 at …") is drawn about 35 px *above* the rect, across the ship ring.
  - The "Assign pilot" / "Fit best" buttons cover the panel's green title bar and the "Hangar" title.
  - The scroll bar sticks up out of the panel.
  - Path: `draw_panel` (`dy`, `brow` and `list` computed from `r` without clamping).
- **D-33 `purchase.items` and `manufacture.jobs` (rect 18,272,538×188):** after the title, detail and
  button reservations only **one row** fits (`visible = 1`).
  - The Market therefore seems to sell only the XWinder; the other items are hidden below the scroll.
  - The title "Market"/"Factory" sits on the art's green header bar instead of inside it.
  - Path: `draw_panel` (`visible`, title `text_in`).
- **D-34 `purchase.funds` / `manufacture.funds` (rect 290,210,140×18, size 12):** the amount is drawn about
  20 px above the dark readout slot in the art (the slot is about y 230–253) and is too small for it.
  - Path: `place_text`; the rect itself in `screens.yaml`.
- **D-35 `research.topics` (rect 330,49,250×262):**
  - The rows (21·k pitch, 15·k text) do not line up with the art's horizontal slot bars (pitch ≈ 21 design
    px but offset), so the text straddles the bars.
  - The title "Research" overlaps the first bar.
  - Rows are cut with "..." ("Frontier Base One is resea…", "Advanced Lasers – 0% (8000…").
  - Only 4 rows fit.
  - Path: `draw_panel`.
