# Stage 2 — Enemies and combat

**Version 0.3.0. Status: planned.**

- AI pilot behaviours per class, selected by rules: fighters (Wraith, Spectre, Phantasm, Shade,
  pirate and corporate hulls), tug (Harbinger: tractor capture), freighter (Spirit: launches
  drones), drones, probes, turrets. Skill from the difficulty tables.
- Full damage model: fore/aft shields → armour → hull; per-system damage and repair; disabled
  ships.
- Power management: beam/shield charge settings A–E, transfers, shield balance.
- Special systems: VCT, cloak, ECM, tractor beam, damage control computer.
- Missile behaviours: Sidestep, Permeator, Enveloping, Parallax decoys, Passive Cloaking,
  Doppelganger, Fusion; point defence.
- Mines; station modules and defence turrets; base hubs (alien base destroyed with its hub).
- Debris chunks (`*-ship*` meshes), XSPT explosion sprites, lens flares.
- Sound effects through `soundfx.ini`.

**Testbed adds**: one arena per enemy type, a mixed dogfight, a base assault, an escort, and an
"AI duel" spectator scenario.
