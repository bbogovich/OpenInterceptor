# Radio voices

Pilots answer on the radio, chatter lines can be spoken, and a computer voice announces the end
of a mission (app 0.6.2). Voices are **rules data**: a mod gives its pilots voices without code,
and a campaign without any recorded speech is simply quiet.

Requirement: [R-MSN-007](../specification/requirements/missions.md#r-msn-007-voices). Every key
with its type: [Mod file reference › `voices`](reference.md#voices), the ship key
[`voice`](reference.md#ships-and-objects), the [`sounds`](reference.md#sounds) names
`mission_accomplished` and `mission_failed`, and chatter in [Missions](missions.md#the-mission).

## Three kinds of voice

| What | Where it is set | When it is heard |
|---|---|---|
| A **pilot's voice** | `voices: {<set>: {<line>: clips}}`, and `voice: <set>` on the ship class | A wingman answers your orders and status requests; another wingman reports one lost. |
| A **spoken chatter line** | `voice: <clip>` on a mission's chatter entry | With the line, when its condition first holds. |
| The **computer voice** | `sounds: {mission_accomplished, mission_failed}` | When the debrief appears: `mission_accomplished` for a win, `mission_failed` for any other result (lose, draw, retreat, killed). |

## A worked example

The `default` campaign has no recorded speech yet. Its Guard pilots answer with short synthesised
radio chirps, generated by `tools/default_assets/sounds.py` like every other default sound:

```yaml
# resources/default/rules/voices.yaml
voices:
  guard_pilot:
    acknowledge: [sounds/radio_ack.wav]
    status: [sounds/radio_status.wav]
    down: [sounds/radio_down.wav]
```

```yaml
# resources/default/rules/ships.yaml (each of comet, meteor, thunderbolt, victory)
ships:
  comet:
    voice: guard_pilot
    # …
```

```yaml
# resources/default/rules/flight.yaml
sounds:
  mission_accomplished: sounds/mission_win.wav
  mission_failed: sounds/mission_fail.wav
```

```yaml
# a chatter line spoken on the radio (resources/default/missions/missions.yaml, colony_defence)
chatter:
  - {when: "time > 3s", line: "@chat.colony_inbound", voice: sounds/radio_status.wav}
```

## Lines a voice can say

| Line | Said by | When |
|---|---|---|
| `acknowledge` | the first of your wingmen whose voice has the line | You give a wing order: fire at will, attack my target, ignore my target, evasive, retreat. |
| `status` | the same | You ask for a status report. |
| `down` | another of your wingmen (never the one lost) | One of your wingmen is destroyed. |
| `engaging`, `kill` | — | Reserved names, not yet said by the engine. |

- A line may list several clips; one is chosen each time, so the radio does not repeat itself.
- Only **your own wingmen** speak: AI pilots with the `wingman` role led by you. Enemies and
  other friendly craft are silent.
- A ship without `voice:`, or a voice without the line, says nothing; the message line still
  shows the order's text.
- Line names are lower-cased; a line the engine does not know is kept and ignored, so a mod can
  prepare lines for later.

## Writing your own

1. Record or generate short clips (WAV; the engine reads PCM and ADPCM
   WAV). Radio speech reads best when band-limited (roughly 300 – 3 000 Hz) with a little noise.
2. Put them in your pack's `sounds/` folder and list them under a voice set in any rules file.
3. Give your ship classes `voice: <set>`. Different factions or squadrons can have different sets.
4. For the computer voice, set `mission_accomplished` and `mission_failed` under `sounds:`.
5. Run the game and give your wingmen orders (`]`, then 1 – 6). A clip that fails to load is
   reported on start-up as a `sound: …` line.

## Using the player's original voices

The `interceptor` campaign maps the original's **computer voice** through its sound cue table:

```yaml
# packs/interceptor/rules/flight.yaml
sounds:
  mission_accomplished: cue:Comp_MissionAccomplished
  mission_failed: cue:Comp_MissionFailed
```

These clips sit six folders deep on the CD; since app 0.6.2 the original-data index reaches eight
levels, so they are found in an installed copy or a CD copy alike.

The original's **pilot chatter** clips (the numbered `FPilot1xx` and `MPilotxxx` files) are not
mapped yet: which line each clip says is not known, and a wrong mapping would have a wingman say
the wrong thing. Until they are identified (see the Open note on
[R-MSN-007](../specification/requirements/missions.md#r-msn-007-voices)), `interceptor` pilots
are silent. A personal addon can map them for its own use with `original:<file>.wav` references
once the lines are known.

## Errors

| Message | Cause |
|---|---|
| `ships.x: unknown voice 'y'` | `voice:` names a set that no `voices:` entry defines. |
| `sound: …` on start-up | A clip that could not be loaded (a wrong path, or original data missing). `--check` does not load voice clips yet. |

!!! note "Fixed in app 0.6.7"
    `cue:<Name>` references now resolve inside `voices:` and chatter `voice:` too, through the
    same cue table as `sounds:` (main `65141dc`). In app 0.6.2 – 0.6.6 they did not; use the asset
    path (`original:<file>.wav`) for those versions.
