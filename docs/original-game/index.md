# The original game

*X-COM: Interceptor* (MicroProse, May 1998) combines a **real-time strategy layer**, where the
player runs X-COM's bases on a frontier of star systems, with a **3D space-combat simulator**,
where the player flies one ship with AI wingmen.

This section records what the engine has to reproduce and how the data on the CD is stored.
Facts carry a source and, where they were worked out rather than read, a confidence:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **verified** | Checked against the data (parsed, rendered, or counted) |
| **derived** | Reasoned from strings, field names or values; not yet proven in play |
| **unknown** | Not yet determined |

Sources: the release `Readme.txt`, the INI files and the string tables on the CD (read as data,
never reproduced here), the 1998 demo readme, a published walkthrough, Wikipedia and MobyGames.
UFOpaedia's Interceptor pages were not reachable by automated tools and remain to be checked.
