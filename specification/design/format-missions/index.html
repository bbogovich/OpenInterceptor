    Format: missions, environments and events¶

    Status: design; implemented in Stage 3 (missions, environments), Stage 4 (generation from the strategy layer) and Stage 5 (campaign events). Stage 3 part 1 (app 0.3.0) implemented mission types: roles, arrivals (as a role's at: / arrive:), waves, objectives, optional objectives, timer, outcomes as checked actions, chatter and debriefs, flown from the launcher — documented for modders on Missions. Hazards followed in app 0.5.1, environments, atmospheric flight, terrain and the atmosphere HUD in 0.6.0 and ground installations in 0.6.1 — documented on Missions › Hazards and Environments and terrain; the notes below mark what was built and how it differs from this design. Still planned: location, backdrop from the map, generated, refuse, roles from: the strategy situation, chatter voices, simulator missions, terrain detail textures and scattered objects, events and squadrons. Files in missions/*.yaml and events/*.yaml (YAML, like every mod file). The scenario format stays for testbed arenas; a mission is a scenario with roles, waves, objectives and outcomes.

    In the original, mission composition is entirely hard-coded (27 briefing types, 7 side missions, 12 simulator types). The interceptor mod writes each one as a mission type below.

    Mission types: missions: {<id>: …}¶

    missions:
  defend_outpost:
    name: "@mission.defend_outpost"
    briefing: "@brief.defend_outpost"            # text with {variables}
    location: outpost                            # location kind on the map
    environment: orbit                           # battle environment (below)
    backdrop: location                           # star, planet, belt of the map location
    generated: "squadron_targets(alien, outpost)"   # when the strategy layer creates it
    refuse: {score: refused_outpost_defence, relation: {owner: -6}}
    # Forces: roles filled from the strategy situation, with fallbacks for the testbed
    roles:
      station: {from: target_outpost, behaviour: station_guns}
      guards: {class: corporate_fighter, count: [2, 4], faction: owner, behaviour: corporate_guard}
      attackers: {from: attacking_squadron}
      player: {from: launched_craft}             # at most the campaign's max_craft_per_mission
    arrivals:
      attackers: {distance: 6km, from_role: station, time: 0s}
    waves:
      - {role: attackers, from: squadron_reserve, when: "time > 120s and alive(station)", at: random_edge}
    # Objectives
    objectives:
      win: "destroyed_all(attackers) or retreated_all(attackers)"
      lose: "destroyed(station)"
    optional:
      capture: "captured_any(attackers)"
    outcomes:
      win: [score(outpost_defended), salvage, "relation(owner, +8)"]
      lose: [lose(outpost), score(outpost_lost)]
      retreat: [score(refused_outpost_defence)]  # hyperspace out
    debrief: "@debrief.defend_outpost.{result}"
    Key Meaning
    location, environment, backdrop Where the battle happens and what the arena is like. environment: <id> implemented (app 0.6.0; missions and scenarios); location and backdrop: location need the strategy map (Stage 4) — a planet backdrop is part of the environment for now.
    generated The strategy-layer condition that creates the mission (squadron meets asset, request, event). Testbed and story missions omit it.
    roles: {<name>: …} A group of objects: from the situation (the squadron that triggered it, the station under attack), from a class list with counts (per difficulty), or the player's launched craft. Each role has a behaviour and a faction. Implemented for class lists and counts (the player's craft is player:); from: the situation is Stage 4.
    arrivals: {<role>: …}, waves: [..] When and where groups enter: time, condition, position (distance and bearing, edge, near another role, hyperspace in). Implemented differently: arrival is part of each role — at: {distance, bearing, from, spread} and arrive: <condition> — not a separate arrivals: map (DEV-040). Waves implemented ({role, when, count, at}, each once). Edge and hyperspace-in arrivals: planned.
    objectives: {win, lose, draw}, optional: {<id>: …} Conditions; checked every tick. draw for draws (the original has win/draw/lose debriefs). Implemented (lose before win; optional objectives latched).
    outcomes: {<result>: [..]} Action lists: score, salvage, capture, relation changes, map changes (outpost lost), flags, story steps. Implemented as checked actions; message runs in battle, campaign actions are listed on the debrief until Stage 4 applies them.
    timer timer: {after: 10min, then: lose} for timed missions (the hijacked Nova Bomb, the arms dealer). Implemented.
    hazards Implemented (app 0.5.1; missions and scenarios), with explicit keys instead of the sketched shorthand: {asteroids: {class, count, radius, center, spin}, minefield: {class, count, radius, center, faction}, black_hole: {position, pull, radius}, ion_storm: {sensors: 40%}} — sensors is the fraction of radar range kept, not a reduction (R-MSN-009).
    chatter Radio lines by trigger. Implemented as - {when: <condition>, line: "@chat.more_bogeys"} (each said once); voices are planned.
    debrief Debrief text per result. Implemented.

    Logistics missions (planned). convoy_ambush (an enemy attacks a supply convoy; destroyed or captured cargo never arrives) and construction_defence (an enemy strikes a facility before it is finished, from 50 % construction and at most three times per location; the facility appears in its construction-stage model) are generated by the strategy layer's logistics; a supply convoy's attack point is chosen at random at its launch (the 25 % attack roll belongs to survey ships); see Logistics.

    Simulator missions (the original's combat simulator) are mission types with simulator: true and parameters chosen in a setup screen (params: {fighters: [1, 12], tech: [1, 3]}), with no campaign effects.

    Battle environments: environments: {<id>: …}¶

    The original fights only in space: deep space, around stations, in asteroid belts and next to a black hole. The format also supports atmospheric and ground battles — attacks on and defence of surface installations such as mining colonies and research outposts. The default campaign uses them; the interceptor campaign does not.

    As built (app 0.6.0 – 0.6.1)

    Built: physics: space | atmosphere, arena, gravity, air_density, ceiling, terrain (the design's ground), an inline sky: {top, horizon, fog, fog_distance, stars} (the design names a sky), backdrop: {planet: {color, size, direction}}; terrains with generate: {kind: craters | hills, seed} or heightmap, a single size (square), height: [low, high] as plain metres, colors: {low, high, slope} and resolution. Open space is simply no environment: (there is no space entry to define). Not built: inherit between environments (the rules' own inherit works), speed_of_sound, weather, the terrain's texture (base and detail maps) and objects (scattered rocks). Reference: Environments and terrain.

    environments:
  space:
    physics: space                      # newtonian: no drag, no gravity (the implemented model)
    arena: 60km                         # sphere radius
    backdrop: [starfield, location_bodies]
  orbit:
    inherit: space
    backdrop: [starfield, {planet: location, distance: 20000km}]
  atmosphere_rocky:
    physics: atmosphere
    gravity: 9.0                        # m/s², downwards (−Y)
    air_density: 1.0                    # scales drag and lift; 0 = vacuum (a moon)
    speed_of_sound: 330                 # optional: buffeting near it
    ground: rocky_moon                  # heightfield (below)
    ceiling: 12km                       # leaving the top = escaping to orbit (retreat)
    sky: dusk_violet                    # gradient, sun, clouds, fog distance
    weather: {dust: 0.3}                # visibility and sensor range factor

terrains:
  rocky_moon:
    heightmap: textures/terrain/rocky_hm.png     # or generate: {kind: craters, seed: 7}
    size: [40km, 40km]
    height: [0m, 2500m]
    texture: {base: textures/terrain/rocky.png, detail: textures/terrain/rock_detail.png}
    objects: {scatter: "models/rock_*.obj", density: 0.002}

    Atmospheric flight¶

    When physics: atmosphere, the flight model adds forces the space model does not have, so the same ship flies very differently on a planet:

    Force Rule Effect
    Drag F = −½ · ρ · Cd · A · v·|v| (ρ = air_density, per-ship drag_area = Cd·A) A ship slows down without thrust; top speed is where thrust equals drag (much lower than in space)
    Gravity gravity along −Y A ship must hold altitude with lift or thrust; a stalled or disabled ship falls and crashes
    Lift lift (per ship) × ρ × v² × angle of attack, perpendicular to velocity; zero for ships without wings Winged craft bank to turn and glide; wingless saucers hover on thrust (hover: true with a power cost)
    Ground Collision with the heightfield; impact_speed below which a ship lands instead of crashing Landing pads, crashes, terrain masking
    Ceiling Above ceiling the battle ends for that ship (escape to orbit = retreat) Replaces hyperspace-out

    Ship rules gain drag_area, lift, hover, atmosphere_max_speed (a cap) and atmospheric: false for craft that cannot enter an atmosphere (the mission screen hides them). Missiles get drag_area too. flight: {model: newtonian} (or assisted; implemented) stays the default for space; an environment's physics overrides it for its battles.

    As built (app 0.6.0)

    The forces were simplified so that each is set by one number a modder can reason about (R-FLT-011):

    • drag a = −k·ρ·|v|·v with k = acceleration / atmosphere_max_speed² — full thrust balances drag at atmosphere_max_speed (default 60 % of max_speed); there is no drag_area, and missiles feel no drag;
    • lift g·ρ·(forward speed / lift)² along the craft's up, capped at 2g, where lift is the speed at which the wings carry the weight (default 120 m/s for fighters, 0 otherwise); no angle-of-attack term;
    • hover: true cancels gravity, with no power cost yet;
    • a landing is any touchdown at no more than 25 m/s into the ground while upright (a fixed LANDING_SPEED, not a per-ship impact_speed); a crash does (speed)² × 0.5 damage;
    • above ceiling the craft leaves the battle (Departed, "LEFT THE ATMOSPHERE");
    • atmospheric is read but not yet acted on (the mission screen comes with Stage 4).

    Ground installations¶

    Ground bases are object classes with kind: ground (static, placed on the terrain at a surface point: position: {surface: [12km, 4km]}) and the usual model sets, modules, turrets (a behaviour with do: turret and ground arcs) and damage. Surface anti-air turrets, shield domes, landing pads (ships launch from them as an arrival), and buildings that are objectives (roles: {colony: {class: mining_colony}}) all use existing constructs.

    As built (app 0.6.1)

    kind: ground exists: such an object is static and is set down on the terrain wherever it is placed (at the ground height plus a quarter of its collision radius); the environment is set before anything is placed. Positions are ordinary [x, y, z] — the y is replaced — rather than position: {surface: …}. Anti-air is a kind: ground class with beams, a turret_arc and a turret behaviour (the default campaign's dominion_flak). Shield domes and landing pads as arrivals are not built.

    Mission types for the surface follow the same format: Defend colony (surface), Raid research outpost, Ground strike, Rescue at the crash site, with location: surface and an atmospheric environment chosen from the map body (environment: body).

    Campaign events: events: {<id>: …}¶

    Events are how story beats, side missions and reactions happen. An event is a condition plus an action list, checked by the campaign clock.

    events:
  alien_message_2:
    arc: {id: doomsday, step: 2}
    when: >
      date >= 2067-03-01 and flag(alien_message_1) and captured(structural_component)
      and research(wraith_examination)
    chance:                                 # per check; empty = certain once `when` holds
    check: daily                            # daily, weekly, monthly, after_battle, on_research
    do:
      - "message(@msg.alien_2, high)"
      - "unlock_research(alien_message_2)"
      - "set_flag(alien_message_2)"
    once: true
  rogue_freighter:
    when: "month <= 6 and not flag(rogue_freighter_done)"
    chance: 15%
    check: monthly
    do: ["start_mission(rogue_freighter, random_outpost_system, 504h)"]   # expires in 21 days
    reward: {money: 250000}                 # applied by the mission's win outcome
    Key Meaning
    when, chance, check When the event may fire: a condition, a probability per check (one value per difficulty allowed), and how often it is checked.
    do Actions: messages and news, missions (start_mission, offer_mission with accept/refuse), unlocks, flags and counters, money, relation changes, map changes (spawn or remove bases and outposts, reveal locations), plan changes, arc steps, endings.
    choices Choices for the player (- {text: "@choice.pay", do: ["add(money, -200000)", "set_flag(paid)"]}) — a dialogue box with buttons.
    arc, once, cooldown Story ordering, single-shot, minimum time between firings.

    The original's side missions (smarms, smnova, smraiders, smrecon, smrescue, smrogue: discrete triggers, confirmed by its cheat codes) are each one event plus one mission type; the alien messages, Doomsday shipments, MacArthur and final mission are the doomsday arc.

    Squadrons and flight plans¶

    The strategy layer moves squadrons (a group of craft with a size class Small 1–3, Medium 4–6, Large 7–9, Huge 10+), convoys and probes on flight plans across the map. When a squadron's plan meets a player asset or player squadron, or a request is raised, a mission of the matching type is generated, with the squadron filling its role. squadrons: {<id>: …} defines compositions per tech level and difficulty; convoys: {<id>: …} defines freighters, tugs and escorts.
